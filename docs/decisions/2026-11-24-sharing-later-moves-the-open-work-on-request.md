# Sharing later moves the open work to the project tracker, on the person's request

Question: How does a person later share planning kept in the personal tracker with the project?

A person asks to share an item, a plan, or a map that sits in the personal tracker. The skills that already create tickets show what would go to the project tracker and create it on one yes. Only open work moves. Where the project tracker keeps planning out, one note on the outcome ticket takes its place.

## What was decided

1. On request only. A person asks to share. No skill shares anything on its own initiative.
2. No skill of its own. One item is shared through `capture`, a plan through `to-tickets`, and a map through `wayfinder`. Each shows its usual review, grouped by tracker, and takes one yes. In a shared project the text of every ticket is shown first, as the record rule says.
3. Only open work moves. The plan or the map, and its open steps or questions, are created in the project tracker. Its body says in one line what was already done. Finished items stay in the personal tracker and are not created again.
4. The personal items afterwards. The plan in the personal tracker is closed with a line saying where it went. A step that stays the person's keeps its personal item, which now carries a link to the new project ticket. A step handed to someone else has its personal item closed.
5. Who takes the new tickets. They are created unassigned, unless the person names who takes them in the review.
6. It follows the tracker doc. Sharing obeys the `What goes here` role like any other creation. At `everything`, points 3 to 5 apply as written.
7. Where the project tracker keeps planning out. At `outcomes`, or against a `Never` line, no tickets are created. One note is added to the outcome ticket, saying what is left to do and what is done, in words a person would write. It is shown first and added on the user's yes. The steps stay in the personal tracker, and the skill offers to close the ones handed over.
8. Nothing is moved back. A ticket that was shared stays in the project tracker. One that should not be there is closed the way that tracker's doc says, never deleted.

## Options considered

- A skill of its own for sharing: not taken. It is one more name to remember, and the reviews it would show already exist.
- Every step created in the project tracker, finished ones as closed tickets: not taken. The team needs what is left, and closed tickets for work nobody saw cannot be taken back.
- The plan written into the repository, with the steps kept personal: not taken. It is one more thing to keep current, and nobody else could take a step.
- Nothing, where the project tracker keeps planning out: not taken. A handover is when the team most needs to see what is left.
- Tickets created there anyway: not taken. It goes against the team's own rule for its tracker.

## What settled it

- The person's choices: only unfinished work moves, and a note where the tracker keeps planning out.
- The decision that what goes on a project tracker is set in its tracker doc.
- The decision that every project ticket assigned to a person has a personal item that links it.

## Not settled here

- The text that changes in `capture`, `to-tickets`, and `wayfinder`. It is written with the work that follows the map.

## Replaces

Nothing.
